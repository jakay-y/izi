## Request for access to NAFDAC's product verification data for a consumer safety application

I am writing to request read-only access to NAFDAC's registered-product
verification data — through an API or an agreed data feed — for **Verifyd**, a
mobile application I am building to help ordinary Nigerians confirm, on the spot,
whether a product has been registered by NAFDAC.

**What Verifyd does.** A user scans or types the NAFDAC registration number on a
product's packaging. Verifyd checks that number and tells the user whether it
belongs to a genuine registered product, and shows the registered product's
details. The goal is simple: put the power to spot fake and unregistered
products into the hands of consumers, pharmacists, and retailers, in seconds,
for free.

**Why I am approaching NAFDAC directly.** Verifyd is only as trustworthy as its
data. Rather than rely on unofficial or scraped sources, I want Verifyd to check
against NAFDAC's own records, so that what the public sees is authoritative and
current. I believe a verification tool that fights counterfeit products should
be built in partnership with the Agency, not around it.

**What I am requesting.**
1. Read-only, programmatic access (an API endpoint, or a regularly updated data
   export) to the registered-product information already published through the
   Greenbook and the NAPAMS verification portal — at minimum: registration
   number, product name, applicant/manufacturer, product category, and
   registration status.
2. Guidance on any terms, approvals, fees, or a data-sharing agreement the
   Agency requires for this kind of access.
3. Where possible, inclusion of all regulated categories (drugs, food,
   cosmetics, medical devices, packaged water, and chemicals), so consumers can
   verify any NAFDAC-regulated product.

**What NAFDAC gains.** With the Agency's consent, Verifyd would share back
anonymised, aggregated intelligence that supports NAFDAC's work — for example,
which registration numbers are being queried most, products users report as
suspected fakes, and the locations where this activity is concentrated. This
gives the Agency an early-warning signal from the market at no cost. Verifyd
would also prominently credit NAFDAC as the source of the data and direct users
to official channels.

**About me.** I am an independent founder at an early stage; Verifyd is a
startup project, not yet an incorporated company. I am ready to complete any
registration, sign any undertaking, or meet any condition the Agency sets as a
prerequisite for access. I would welcome the chance to demonstrate the
application to your team.

I would be grateful for the opportunity to discuss this. I can be reached at
